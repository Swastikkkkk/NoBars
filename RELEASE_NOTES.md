Offline mesh chat, live location and SOS for iPhone and Android. No internet, no cell signal, no server.

**Downloads**
- `NoBars.apk`: Android 10+. Open it on the phone to install.
- `NoBars-unsigned.ipa`: iPhone, iOS 26+. Sideload with Sideloadly or AltStore (see the README).

**What's in it**
- Multi-hop chat, live location and SOS between iPhones and Android phones
- Keeps relaying in the background over Bluetooth LE, with notifications
- iPhone calls through other phones when your buddy is out of direct range
- Group code never broadcast; every packet encrypted and signed per phone
- Chat history and queued messages survive restarts
